# Roadmap: ideas for the next round

Not built yet, roughly in priority order. Tell us what to add, drop or reorder.

1. Register several people in one order (families, groups), and guest checkout without an account
2. Self-service edits, transfers to another person, and switching races
3. Refunds through the payment processors (now: mark cancelled and refund manually)
4. Waitlist when a race sells out
5. Add-ons and merchandise (extra shirts, parking, medals)
6. Age-group categories calculated automatically for results
7. Team fundraising pages and leaderboards
8. Printable bib labels and check-in by QR code
9. Volunteer sign-up
10. SMS/WhatsApp notifications
11. Multi-language (Spanish, French, Dutch for the wider Caribbean)
12. Organizer payouts report and invoice for platform fees
13. Postgres, background job queue for bulk email, and file storage for logos and course maps
